Platform

8 modules. One mission.
Clean air for India.

Every module is backed by research from WHO, NASA, FAO, and IPCC. This isn't another feel-good app — it's environmental intelligence.

🌍
Core Module
Air Quality Intelligence Hub
Real-time PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃ monitoring for any location in India. Not just numbers — personalized action plans that tell you exactly what to do when the air gets dangerous. Auto-generates alerts and health recommendations based on your sensitivity profile.
42
AQI
● Live Monitoring
🧠
AI Engine
Smart Plant Recommendations
7-factor matching algorithm analyzes your climate zone, soil type, sunlight hours, available space, air quality goals, and personal preferences to recommend the perfect species from 30+ native plants.
🌱
Tracking
My Garden Lifecycle Tracker
Personal plant journal with growth stage monitoring, watering schedules, health diagnostics, and photo documentation. Watch your green impact grow over time with detailed progress visualizations.
🏠
Home Air Score
15-question assessment that evaluates your living space and generates a personalized indoor air quality score with actionable plant-based recommendations to purify your home.
📊
Impact Quantifier
Real-time calculation of your environmental impact — CO₂ absorbed, O₂ produced, pollutants filtered — with 10-year projections. Numbers that make your contribution tangible and shareable.
🩺
AI Plant Doctor
Upload a photo of your ailing plant and get instant diagnosis. Identifies diseases, nutrient deficiencies, and care issues with treatment recommendations.
🎮
Gamified Engagement
XP system, achievement badges, daily streaks, and competitive leaderboards turn environmental action into an addictive habit loop. School competitions, class rankings, and social sharing built in.
Process

Four steps to a
greener planet.

From first click to first tree — Filtree makes environmental action effortless and data-driven.

01
Enter Your Location
Drop your pin. We instantly analyze your climate zone, soil type, and real-time air quality.
02
Get AI Recommendations
Our 7-factor algorithm matches you with the perfect species — outdoor trees or balcony plants.
03
Plant & Track
Follow step-by-step planting guides. Log growth, set reminders, photograph milestones.
04
Watch Your Impact
See real-time CO₂ offset, O₂ generated, and pollutants filtered. Earn XP. Climb leaderboards.
